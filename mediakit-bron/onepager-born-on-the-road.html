Born on the road

One family, nine months, three chapters. We are Auke and Yummee, travelling with Emma (6) and Elin (4) — and in April 2027 our third child is born on Bali, mid-journey. We are documenting the whole arc in English and Dutch: travelling pregnant, giving birth abroad, and the first months on the road with a newborn. For a pregnancy or baby brand, that is not a one-off video — it is a story your audience follows, with your product inside every chapter.
